# ⚡ Thermoelectric Energy Recovery from Automobile Heat

## Overview

A project exploring the recovery of otherwise wasted automobile exhaust heat using a thermoelectric generator (TEG).

## Principle

The project is based on the **Seebeck effect**: when a temperature difference exists across a thermoelectric module, an electrical potential is generated.

```text
Automobile Exhaust Heat
          ↓
   Hot-side heat transfer
          ↓
       TEG Module
          ↓
     DC Electrical Output
          ↓
   Conditioning / Storage
          ↓
     Useful Electrical Load
```

## Main Focus

- Utilization of waste heat
- Thermoelectric power generation
- Automotive energy recovery
- Electrical energy storage / conditioning
- Improving the usefulness of otherwise wasted thermal energy

## Key Concepts

`Seebeck Effect` `Thermoelectric Generator` `Waste Heat Recovery` `Automotive Engineering` `Energy Conversion`

## Engineering Challenge

TEG performance depends strongly on the temperature difference, thermal interfaces, heat transfer, electrical loading and thermal management. A practical design therefore requires both thermal and electrical optimization.
